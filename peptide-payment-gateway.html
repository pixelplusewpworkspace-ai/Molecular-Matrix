Frequently Asked Questions

What payment processors work with peptide businesses?

Only specialized high-risk payment processors and acquiring banks work with peptide businesses. Mainstream gateways like Stripe, PayPal, or Square do not allow peptide sales and will eventually close your account. We partner with specialized banks that explicitly underwrite the peptide industry.

Why do peptide websites get rejected by normal payment gateways?

Peptides fall under a highly regulated category. Processors classify them as high-risk due to FDA regulations, the potential for non-research human consumption, and high chargeback rates. Standard gateways avoid this risk entirely, leading to swift rejections or frozen funds for peptide merchants.

How long does high-risk merchant approval take?

For a peptide website, the underwriting process is more rigorous than standard accounts. Once all required compliance documents are submitted, approval typically takes between 7-14 business days, depending on the specific acquiring bank's review process.

Do you also build the website, or just handle payment approval?

We can do both. While we specialize in securing high-risk merchant accounts and integrating the gateway, Molecular Matrix is a full-service web agency. We can build your peptide website from scratch or simply integrate the payment solution into your existing site.
